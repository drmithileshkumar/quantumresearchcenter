Research topic

Quantum Communication and Networks

Physical links, quantum memories, and network protocols for distributing quantum information.

Research topics

Explore a keyword to see its research overview and more specific topics.

  • Quantum repeaters and network scaling

Scope and methods

Quantum communication technology concerns the physical realization of protocols between separated systems. Sources, channels, memories, and local operations jointly determine the quality and rate of the distributed quantum resource.

Network research combines these components into architectures that tolerate loss, limited memory lifetimes, and uncertain link generation. The underlying entanglement and communication limits are studied in Quantum Information Theory; this branch concerns their realization at a system level.

Selected references
