Research topic

Measurement-induced quantum phases

Entanglement transitions in monitored many-body systems and their experimental signatures.

Scope and methods

In monitored many-body systems, coherent dynamics can create entanglement while measurements reshape or remove it. Varying the monitoring can lead to qualitatively different patterns of entanglement in individual measurement-conditioned evolutions.

Connecting these theoretical distinctions to experiments requires suitable observables and careful treatment of finite size, postselection, and device noise. These issues connect monitored dynamics to statistical mechanics and quantum information theory.

Open problems

  • Which signatures distinguish a transition from a finite-size or noise-induced crossover?
  • How can measurement-conditioned phenomena be probed without prohibitive data or postselection costs?
