Research topic

Many-body dynamics

Entanglement dynamics, transport, relaxation, and measurement in interacting quantum systems.

Scope and methods

Many-body dynamics studies what happens when an interacting system evolves, is driven, or is measured. The growth of entanglement connects this physics to both the difficulty of classical simulation and the possibilities of quantum experiments.

Measurement is itself an intervention. Repeated observations can compete with entangling dynamics, so the measurement protocol becomes part of the model rather than simply a passive way to inspect it.
