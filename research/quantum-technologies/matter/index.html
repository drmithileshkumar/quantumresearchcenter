Research topic

Quantum matter and simulation platforms

Interacting quantum systems, phases of matter, and controlled platforms for quantum simulation.

Research topics

Explore a keyword to see its research overview and more specific topics.

  • Many-body dynamics
  • Measurement-induced quantum phases

Scope and methods

When many quantum degrees of freedom interact, collective behavior emerges that is not evident from one isolated constituent. Correlations, phases, and dynamics connect condensed-matter questions to quantum information.

Controlled platforms make selected models experimentally accessible. The challenge is to determine which features of a model survive preparation, control, readout, and finite system size.
