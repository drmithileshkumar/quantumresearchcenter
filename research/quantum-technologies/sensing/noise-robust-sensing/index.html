Research topic

Noise-resilient quantum sensing

Quantum sensing protocols that retain sensitivity in the presence of decoherence and control errors.

Scope and methods

Error suppression in sensing must preserve the parameter-dependent evolution that carries the signal. Error correction can help only under suitable conditions on the signal, noise, and available operations.

Another approach exploits enhanced response near critical behavior. Evaluating either strategy requires counting preparation and measurement time as well as noise and control overhead.

Open problems

  • Which experimentally available controls suppress noise without erasing the parameter being estimated?
  • Does an apparent precision gain survive preparation time, readout cost, and model uncertainty?

Selected references
