Research topic

Benchmarking under realistic noise

Device benchmarking with correlated errors, drift, and context-dependent noise.

Scope and methods

Benchmarking summarizes the behavior of an operation or device through a specified experiment. Its result is useful only to the extent that the experiment and its assumptions capture the behavior relevant to the intended task.

Research addresses statistically efficient detection of departures from assumed noise models. Different metrics can be complementary; a favorable average value does not by itself establish reliable performance for every computation.

Open problems

  • How can limited data distinguish slow drift, correlated errors, and sampling fluctuations?
  • Which benchmark quantities predict performance on a specified application or logical operation?

Selected references

  1. Robust randomized benchmarking of quantum processes

    Easwar Magesan, J. M. Gambetta, Joseph Emerson. 2010. Research paper

    Develops a protocol for estimating average gate errors with stated assumptions about noise.

  2. Quantum error correction below the surface code threshold

    Rajeev Acharya et al.. 2024. Experimental research

    Reports surface-code memories with improving logical performance as code distance increases in the tested regime.
