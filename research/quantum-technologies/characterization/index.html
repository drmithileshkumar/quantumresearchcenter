Research topic

Characterization and experimental validation

Estimation and validation of quantum states, operations, and device noise from experimental data.

Research topics

Explore a keyword to see its research overview and more specific topics.

  • Benchmarking under realistic noise

Scope and methods

Characterization connects experimental records to a model of a state, operation, or device. The chosen protocol determines which properties can be estimated and which assumptions are needed to interpret them.

Average gate error does not determine the full noise process. Experimental validation combines a suitable protocol with uncertainty estimates, checks for drift, and comparisons against independently motivated predictions.

Selected references

  1. Robust randomized benchmarking of quantum processes

    Easwar Magesan, J. M. Gambetta, Joseph Emerson. 2010. Research paper

    Develops a protocol for estimating average gate errors with stated assumptions about noise.

  2. Self-testing of quantum systems: a review

    Ivan Šupić, Joseph Bowles. 2019. Review

    Explains how observed correlations can constrain a system’s underlying state and measurements, under explicit assumptions.
