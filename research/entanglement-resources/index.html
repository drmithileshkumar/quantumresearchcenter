3.2 Entanglement resource theory

Detection, quantification, and conversion of bipartite and multipartite entanglement.

Scope and methods

Entanglement resource theory characterizes states and their transformations relative to a specified class of allowed operations. Detection establishes whether entanglement is present; quantification and conversion address the rates or costs of particular information-processing tasks.

Research includes mixed-state and multipartite entanglement, computable bounds on conversion rates, and relations between resource measures and experimentally accessible witnesses. The relevant measure depends on the operational task and available resources.

Open problems

  • Which experimentally accessible quantities give useful bounds on entanglement-assisted tasks?
  • How can conversion and distribution of multipartite entanglement be characterized with manageable resources?
