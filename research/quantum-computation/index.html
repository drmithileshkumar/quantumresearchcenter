Scope and methods

Quantum computing studies how physical evolution can perform a computation and what resources that computation requires. Its theoretical questions concern algorithm design, simulation, complexity, and the protection of logical information.

Algorithms and error correction form complementary branches: one concerns the procedure for solving a problem; the other concerns its reliability under imperfect operations. Programming research addresses representation and execution, while processor technologies address physical integration.
