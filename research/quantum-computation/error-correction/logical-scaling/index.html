Research topic

Scalable fault-tolerant computation

Logical error rates, gate implementation, and resource requirements for scalable fault-tolerant computation.

Scope and methods

A logical memory tests how well encoded information survives repeated error-correction cycles. A useful processor must additionally prepare, operate on, and read out logical states without losing that protection.

Below-threshold memory results are important evidence for scaling, but complete computation brings further challenges: correlated errors, leakage, decoding latency, and the resources needed for logical gates.

Open problems

  • How does logical performance change when protected operations replace idle memory cycles?
  • How can decoding, calibration, and control keep pace with a growing processor?

Selected references

    Rajeev Acharya et al.. 2024. Experimental research
