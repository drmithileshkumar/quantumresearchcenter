Research topic

Variational quantum algorithms

Trainability, sampling complexity, and noise sensitivity of variational quantum algorithms.

Scope and methods

A variational algorithm adjusts a circuit by repeatedly estimating an objective and updating parameters with a classical optimizer. Its success depends on more than circuit expressiveness: noise, sampling, initialization, and the optimization landscape all matter.

Performance analysis requires explicit noise models, measurement budgets, optimization costs, and classical comparison methods. Numerical results on small instances do not establish asymptotic speedup; scaling claims require separate complexity analysis.

Open problems

  • Which circuit structures make learning or optimization feasible as a problem grows?
  • When measurement and optimization costs are included, which tasks remain competitive with strong classical methods?
