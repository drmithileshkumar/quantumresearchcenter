Research topic

Quantum program verification

Formal specifications, equivalence checking, and correctness proofs for quantum programs.

Scope and methods

Program verification relates an implementation to a precise statement of its intended behavior. For circuits, it can establish that a transformation preserves the represented computation instead of relying solely on example executions.

Research addresses the scalability of specifications, proof construction, and proof maintenance. A verified transformation only establishes the properties represented in its formal model; physical device behavior requires additional evidence.

Open problems

  • How can verification handle richer measurement and classical-control behavior at useful scales?
  • How should approximate optimizations state and compose their correctness guarantees?
