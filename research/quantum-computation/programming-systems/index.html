Quantum languages and software systems

Quantum programming languages, intermediate representations, semantics, and compiler correctness.

Scope and methods

Quantum programming languages and intermediate representations specify operations on quantum states together with measurement and classical control. Their semantics define program behavior and the equivalences that a compiler must preserve.

Research addresses expressive language design, efficient representation, optimization, and verification. Hardware-specific compilation adds constraints on supported operations, connectivity, and timing; these constraints must be incorporated without invalidating the program specification.
