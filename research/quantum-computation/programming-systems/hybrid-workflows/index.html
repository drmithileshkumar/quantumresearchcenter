Research topic

Reproducible hybrid quantum workflows

Execution models, data provenance, and reproducibility of quantum-classical computations.

Scope and methods

A hybrid workflow combines circuit generation, quantum evaluations, classical optimization, and analysis. Software behavior depends on the interaction of these stages, not solely on the mathematical algorithm at its center.

Programming research asks how to preserve experiment configuration, measurement conventions, numerical settings, and provenance across implementations. Reproducibility requires specifications of simulator settings, device configurations, and execution environments.

Open problems

  • Which execution records are sufficient to reproduce and explain a hybrid result?
  • How can equivalent workflows be tested across different circuit representations and target environments?
