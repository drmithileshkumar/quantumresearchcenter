Research topic

Hardware-aware compilation and routing

Circuit decomposition, qubit mapping, and routing under hardware constraints.

Scope and methods

A circuit may specify interactions that a physical processor cannot perform directly. Compilation decomposes operations and assigns logical qubits to physical locations, adding routing operations when required.

The research problem is to optimize a meaningful cost while respecting correctness and device restrictions. Gate count, depth, execution time, and estimated error can favor different implementations, so benchmark conditions must be explicit.

Open problems

  • Which cost models predict execution quality across varying device conditions?
  • How can mapping and routing scale while retaining useful guarantees or strong comparison baselines?
