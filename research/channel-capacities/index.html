3.3 Quantum channels and communication limits

Achievable communication rates and capacity bounds for noisy quantum channels.

Scope and methods

A quantum channel describes a physical transformation of quantum states. A capacity expresses an achievable communication rate under specified resources, error criteria, and assumptions about repeated uses of that transformation.

The challenge is to connect these operational definitions with quantities that can actually be computed or bounded. Correlated noise and finite-use settings require care because familiar memoryless and asymptotic descriptions need not capture them.

Open problems

  • Which bounds remain useful for finite uses of channels with memory?
  • How can tractable channel models retain the noise correlations relevant to communication?

Selected references

  1. The quantum capacity of channels with arbitrarily correlated noise

    Francesco Buscemi, Nilanjana Datta. 2009. Research paper

    Studies bounds on quantum communication rates in settings that include correlated noise.

  2. Quantum Non-Markovianity: Characterization, Quantification and Detection

    Ángel Rivas, Susana F. Huelga, Martin B. Plenio. 2014. Review

    Compares ways to define and detect memory in open quantum dynamics.
