Research topic

Quantum Foundations

The mathematical and conceptual foundations of quantum states, measurement, correlations, and dynamics.

Research topics

  • Device-independent certification

Scope and methods

Quantum foundations studies the mathematical and conceptual assumptions of quantum mechanics. Principal questions concern the representation of physical states, the measurement process, the structure of correlations, and the relation between quantum and classical descriptions.

Operational predictions and their interpretation are distinct objects of study. Bell inequalities constrain local hidden-variable explanations of correlations. Decoherence and open-system models describe the influence of an environment on observable interference and reduced dynamics.
