Entanglement

Entanglement detection, conversion, and its use in quantum communication protocols.

Research topics

Explore a keyword to see its research overview and more specific topics.

  • Entanglement resource theory

Scope and methods

Entanglement is a property of a composite quantum state. Its operational value depends on the allowed local operations, available classical communication, and the protocol under consideration.

Teleportation illustrates the distinction: transferring an unknown state requires both shared entanglement and classical communication. Long-distance networking adds physical constraints such as loss, finite memory lifetimes, and imperfect operations.
