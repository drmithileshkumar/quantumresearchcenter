Research topic

Non-Markovian quantum processes

Temporal correlations, non-Markovian dynamics, and the reconstruction of multi-time quantum processes.

Scope and methods

When a system remains correlated with its environment, its later behavior can depend on earlier interventions. Comparing states at only two times may not capture all of that dependence.

Multi-time process descriptions organize both the dynamics and the experimental operations used to probe it. Research must balance expressive models against the number of measurements and computational resources needed to reconstruct them.

Open problems

  • Which memory features can be identified with a tractable number of interventions?
  • How can a reduced model retain the memory relevant to prediction or control?

Selected references

  1. Operational Markov condition for quantum processes

    Felix A. Pollock et al.. 2018. Research paper

    Connects memory to interventions performed at multiple times in a quantum process.

  2. Quantum Non-Markovianity: Characterization, Quantification and Detection

    Ángel Rivas, Susana F. Huelga, Martin B. Plenio. 2014. Review

    Compares ways to define and detect memory in open quantum dynamics.
