Scope and methods

Quantum information theory studies information encoded in quantum states and processed through quantum operations. It characterizes state distinguishability, entanglement, communication rates, and transformations under specified resource constraints.

Operational tasks provide definitions of quantities such as channel capacity and distillable entanglement. Research seeks achievable protocols, converse bounds, and computable characterizations, including finite-resource settings and correlated noise.
