Introduction

This handbook organizes research topics in quantum science around their theoretical basis, methods, and open problems. It covers quantum foundations and information, computation and error correction, experimental systems, programming, and physical technologies.

The chapters provide concise research overviews and annotated references. They are intended for readers with a background in quantum mechanics and linear algebra who need an entry point into a topic or its literature.

Scope of this reference

The current entries are research overviews, not exhaustive surveys. Open questions identify directions of investigation; cited papers should be consulted for precise assumptions, proofs, and experimental results.

Organization

The four parts separate theoretical questions, experimental methods, programming systems, and device architectures. Numbered sections treat more specific problems within each chapter. These divisions are organizational: methods and results frequently apply across them.
