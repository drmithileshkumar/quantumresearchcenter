A guide, not a league table

How we describe quality

Categories are editorial judgments based on the series’ track record, scientific focus, programme, and organizers. They are not official rankings, acceptance-rate estimates, or guarantees of an individual talk’s quality. Industry forums serve a different purpose from research conferences.

Flagship research
Editorial selection of long-running, field-defining research meetings with a dedicated international scientific community.
Established research
Recurring scientific meetings with a documented academic programme, committees, or institutional backing.
Emerging / regional
Institution-hosted or regional meetings with a relevant scientific programme; a shorter or more local track record.
Industry forum
Business and ecosystem meetings. Evaluate these for partnerships and applications, separately from research prestige.

This is a selective directory, not a complete global calendar. Each entry links to its organizer or institutional source. Dates pending means that an edition is announced but exact dates were not confirmed in the cited source.
