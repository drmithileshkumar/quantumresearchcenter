Classifying bipartite entanglement

There is no single classification of two-party entanglement. Each idea here sorts states by a different question: what are the parties, what can they do, what can a test see, what can the state be used for, and how much of it is there? This map gathers those ideas and shows how they cut across one another.

Every idea, in outline

  • What counts as a party Topic

    Before any state can be classified, the system has to be split in two. That split is usually taken for granted, but it is a choice, and it is not always a tensor product of two Hilbert spaces.

    P. Zanardi 2003

    • A tensor product structure is induced by accessible observables Established

      Which subsystems exist depends on which observables can be controlled and measured, so entanglement is relative to that choice rather than a property of a state vector alone.

      P. Zanardi 2003

    • Slater rank replaces Schmidt rank for two fermions Established

      Two-fermion pure states have a Slater decomposition and a Slater rank, analogous to the Schmidt decomposition and rank. Mixed states are then sorted by their Slater number, the smallest Slater rank needed to build them.

      J. Schliemann 2000 · K. Eckert 2002

    • Mode entanglement and particle entanglement differ Established

      With indistinguishable particles, entanglement can be assigned to modes or restricted to what parties can actually extract. The operational quantity is bounded by the mode-occupation value, and a single particle shared across two modes has none of it.

      P. Zanardi 2001 · H. M. Wiseman 2002

    • Infinite-dimensional systems need more careful definitions Established

      In infinite dimensions, states with infinite entropy of entanglement are trace-norm dense, so every product state has arbitrarily entangled neighbours. A bound on mean energy restores sensible behaviour.

      J. Eisert 2001

    • In quantum field theory, regions do not factorise Established

      Entanglement here is a property of the algebra of observables, not only of states. Regions and their complements do not give a tensor product of two Hilbert spaces, so entropy needs more careful treatment.

      E. Witten 2018

    • Tensor-product and commuting-operator correlations differ Established

      Separation can be modelled by a tensor product or by commuting operators on one space. MIP* = RE implies the two give different correlation sets, a negative answer to Tsirelson's problem.

      Z. Ji 2020

  • Pure states are classified by their Schmidt coefficients Topic

    A bipartite pure state is summarised by its Schmidt coefficients. Each notion of equivalence reads a different amount from that list: all of it, its length, its majorization order, or only its entropy.

    R. Horodecki 2007 · M. A. Nielsen 1998

    • Local unitaries preserve exactly the Schmidt coefficients Established

      Two bipartite pure states are related by local unitaries exactly when they have the same Schmidt coefficients. This is the finest classification on the page.

      R. Horodecki 2007

    • Stochastic LOCC classes are labelled by Schmidt rank Established

      If conversion only has to succeed with non-zero probability, a pure state can reach another exactly when its Schmidt rank is at least as large. Mutual convertibility therefore means equal rank.

      W. Dür 2000 · H.-K. Lo 1997 · G. Vidal 1999

    • Deterministic LOCC gives a partial order Topic

      With LOCC and certainty required, Schmidt coefficients are compared by majorization. The result is an order, not a list of classes.

      M. A. Nielsen 1998

      • Majorization decides deterministic pure state conversion Established

        A pure state converts to another by LOCC exactly when its Schmidt coefficients are majorized by those of the target.

        M. A. Nielsen 1998

      • Some pure states cannot be converted in either direction Established

        Majorization is only a partial order, so there are pairs of states neither of which can deterministically reach the other. No single number can capture single-copy convertibility.

        M. A. Nielsen 1998 · G. Vidal 1998

      • A finite set of monotones gives the optimal conversion probability Established

        When conversion cannot be certain, its best probability is set by a finite family of monotones built from the Schmidt coefficients. These quantify the entanglement of a single copy.

        G. Vidal 1999

    • Catalysts coarsen the order Topic

      Allowing an entangled state that is returned at the end enlarges what is reachable. The order relaxes in stages, ending at a single number.

      D. Jonathan 1999 · T. V. Kondra 2021

      • Catalysts enable conversions majorization forbids Established

        Some transformations ruled out by Nielsen's theorem become possible with a catalyst that is recovered intact. The resulting relation between Schmidt vectors is called trumping.

        D. Jonathan 1999

      • Explicit inequalities decide the trumping relation Established

        Turgut and Klimesh independently gave complete families of inequalities that decide whether one Schmidt vector can trump another.

        S. Turgut 2007 · M. Klimesh 2007

      • With correlated catalysts, entropy alone decides conversion Established

        If the catalyst may end up correlated with the system, entanglement entropy completely characterises pure state transformations, even for a single copy.

        T. V. Kondra 2021

    • Asymptotically, entropy is the only invariant Established

      With many copies, pure states convert reversibly at a rate set by the entropy of the reduced state. All the structure of the Schmidt list collapses to one number.

      C. H. Bennett 1995 · M. J. Donald 2001

  • The geometry of mixed states Topic

    Mixed states form a convex body with the separable states inside it. Classifying a state can mean locating it in that body: by the sets it belongs to, its orbit under local operations, or its distance from the boundary.

    R. F. Werner 1989 · K. Zyczkowski 1998

    • The separable states are a convex set Established

      Mixtures of product states are closed under further mixing, so any entangled state can be separated from the set by a hyperplane. That geometric fact is what makes entanglement witnesses exist.

      R. F. Werner 1989 · M. Horodecki 1996

    • How large is the separable set? Topic

      The separable set has non-zero volume, but how much of state space it fills depends strongly on dimension and on how states are drawn.

      K. Zyczkowski 1998

      • The separable states have positive volume Established

        Separable states are not a negligible set: they occupy a positive fraction of the state space in any finite dimension.

        K. Zyczkowski 1998

      • A ball of separable states surrounds the maximally mixed state Established

        All states close enough to the maximally mixed state are separable. Gurvits and Barnum bound the largest such ball.

        L. Gurvits 2002

      • Random induced states change from entangled to separable sharply Established

        For a random state on two d-dimensional systems, obtained by tracing out an environment of dimension s, there is a sharp threshold of order roughly d³. Below it the state is very probably entangled, above it very probably separable.

        G. Aubrun 2011

    • What can the eigenvalues alone decide? Topic

      Some states are separable however they are rotated globally. For those, the eigenvalues are enough, and no entangling unitary can help.

      M. Kus 2000 · S. Arunachalam 2014

      • Absolutely separable states stay separable under every global unitary Established

        Kuś and Życzkowski defined this class, in which every state with the same spectrum is separable.

        M. Kus 2000

      • Absolutely PPT states have a spectral characterisation Established

        Hildebrand showed that whether every rotation of a state remains PPT can be decided from its eigenvalues, using a finite set of linear matrix inequalities.

        R. Hildebrand 2005

      • For qubit-qudit systems, absolutely separable equals absolutely PPT Established

        In 2×n systems, the two spectral classes coincide, so the question is answered by a simple condition on the eigenvalues.

        N. Johnston 2013

      • Does absolutely PPT imply absolutely separable in every dimension? Open

        The two sets coincide in the qubit-qudit case, and absolutely separable states are always absolutely PPT. Whether the converse holds generally is the question.

        Still unknown: The general equivalence has not been proved or refuted. Arunachalam, Johnston and Russo show that a wide range of witnesses and positive maps cannot separate the two sets.

        S. Arunachalam 2014

    • Sorting mixed states by local orbits and normal forms Topic

      Two mixed states count as the same if local operations relate them. Invariants and normal forms label these orbits, in place of the Schmidt list that pure states enjoy.

      M. Kus 2000 · F. Verstraete 2001

      • Local orbits can differ in dimension Established

        Explicit methods compute the dimension of the local orbit of any mixed state, which characterises the set of effectively different states.

        M. Kus 2000

      • Polynomial invariants label local unitary classes Established

        Local unitary classes of mixed states can be separated by polynomial invariants of the density matrix. Makhlin gave a full set for mixed states of two qubits.

        M. Grassl 1997 · Y. Makhlin 2000

      • Local filtering brings states to a normal form Established

        Invertible local filters generalise the singular value decomposition to mixed states. For two qubits this is a Lorentz singular value decomposition, which gives a concrete analysis of separability.

        F. Verstraete 2001 · F. Verstraete 2001 · J. M. Leinaas 2006

      • A complete picture of two-qubit states with maximally mixed subsystems Established

        Written in the Hilbert-Schmidt basis, two-qubit states with maximally disordered subsystems admit a complete characterisation of which are inseparable.

        R. Horodecki 1996

    • Every state splits into a separable part and an entangled remainder Established

      A state can be decomposed with the largest possible separable weight, leaving an entangled remainder. This gives a way to compare states by how much separable part they contain.

      M. Lewenstein 1997

    • The Schmidt number sorts mixed states into nested sets Topic

      Schmidt rank extends to mixed states as a ladder of nested convex sets, in which separable states are only the first rung.

      B. M. Terhal 1999

      • The Schmidt number extends rank to mixed states Established

        A mixed state's Schmidt number is the smallest k such that it can be built from pure states of Schmidt rank at most k, minimised over all decompositions. Separable states have Schmidt number 1.

        B. M. Terhal 1999 · A. Sanpera 2000

      • k-positive maps detect Schmidt number Established

        A state has Schmidt number at most k exactly when k-positive maps send it to a positive operator. This extends the positive-map test for separability up the ladder.

        B. M. Terhal 1999

      • Witnesses can certify a lower bound on Schmidt number Established

        Sanpera, Bruß and Lewenstein give a canonical form for Schmidt-number witnesses, along with methods to optimise them.

        A. Sanpera 2000

      • Two-qutrit PPT states have Schmidt number at most 2 Established

        Sanpera, Bruß and Lewenstein gave strong evidence for this. Yang, Leung and Tang, and independently Choi, proved that every 2-positive map from M3 to M3 is decomposable, which is the dual statement.

        A. Sanpera 2000 · Y. Yang 2016

  • What separability tests can see Topic

    Every test divides states into those it flags and those it misses, so the tests themselves classify states. Deciding separability exactly is NP-hard, so the useful question is which test sees which states.

    O. Gühne 2008

    • Positive maps classify entanglement by what they can detect Topic

      A positive but not completely positive map applied to one party can turn an entangled state into something with a negative eigenvalue. The structure of these maps mirrors the structure of entanglement.

      M. Horodecki 1996

      • Positive maps collectively decide separability Established

        A state is separable exactly when it stays positive under every positive map applied to one party. The test is complete, but no general description of all positive maps exists.

        M. Horodecki 1996

      • The transpose gives the partial transpose test Established

        The transpose is the most familiar positive map. It splits states into PPT and NPT, and PPT states cannot be distilled. Whether every NPT state can be is still open.

        A. Peres 1996 · M. Horodecki 1996

      • The reduction criterion ties detection to distillation Established

        The reduction map gives a separability test, and a state that violates it can be distilled. Cerf, Adami and Gingrich reached the same criterion through a conditional operator.

        M. Horodecki 1997 · N. J. Cerf 1997

      • Decomposable maps cannot detect PPT entanglement Established

        A decomposable map adds nothing beyond the partial transpose. Detecting PPT entangled states requires an indecomposable map, the first example of which came from Choi. In 2×2 and 2×3, every positive map is decomposable.

        M.-D. Choi 1975 · S. L. Woronowicz 1976 · M. Horodecki 1996

      • Families of indecomposable maps reveal bound entanglement Established

        Explicit families of non-decomposable positive maps detect PPT entangled states in even dimensions and in 2^N×2^N systems. Each family reveals a different slice of them.

        H.-P. Breuer 2006 · W. Hall 2006 · M. Piani 2004

      • Witnesses can be optimised against the separable set Established

        Lewenstein and coauthors give necessary and sufficient conditions for a witness to be optimal. They construct non-decomposable witnesses from edge states, which ties the structure of witnesses to that of PPT entanglement.

        M. Lewenstein 2000

      • What is the general structure of positive maps? Open

        A full description of positive maps would solve the separability problem through the Horodecki criterion, so the two questions are closely tied.

        Still unknown: No general characterisation of positive maps is known in dimensions where indecomposable maps exist. Since deciding separability is NP-hard, a simple one is not expected.

        R. Horodecki 2007 · O. Gühne 2008

    • Tests from rearranged matrices and correlations Topic

      A second family of tests needs no positive map. It bounds a norm or a covariance that separable states cannot exceed, and often catches PPT entangled states.

      O. Gühne 2006

      • The computable cross norm and realignment criterion Established

        Realigning the density matrix and summing its singular values gives a quantity at most 1 on separable states.

        O. Rudolph 2002 · K. Chen 2002

      • Covariance matrices unify several criteria Established

        Conditions on covariance matrices of local observables give strong criteria that detect many bound entangled states, are necessary and sufficient for two qubits, and clarify how earlier criteria are related.

        O. Gühne 2006

      • Violating local uncertainty relations signals entanglement Established

        Separable states inherit uncertainty bounds from their local parts. Hofmann and Takeuchi show that violating such a sum bound is a signature of entanglement.

        H. F. Hofmann 2002

      • The correlation tensor gives a separability condition Established

        Writing a state in terms of local generators gives a correlation tensor. Bounding its norm yields separability conditions and lower bounds on concurrence.

        J. I. d. Vicente 2006

    • Tests comparing global and local disorder Topic

      Separable states cannot be more ordered globally than locally. Entropy and majorization turn that intuition into tests.

      M. A. Nielsen 2000

      • Negative conditional entropy implies entanglement Established

        Inseparability is linked to conditional α-entropy inequalities: a state more disordered locally than globally cannot be separable.

        R. Horodecki 1996 · N. J. Cerf 1997

      • Separable states are more disordered globally than locally Established

        For a separable state, the global spectrum is majorized by each local spectrum. This is stronger than the entropy inequality alone.

        M. A. Nielsen 2000

    • Tests based on range, rank and product vectors Topic

      A separable state's range is spanned by product vectors whose local conjugates span the range of its partial transpose. Rank and range thus classify PPT states that spectral tests cannot reach.

      P. Horodecki 1997

      • The range criterion detects PPT entangled states Established

        The range condition gave the first explicit PPT entangled states, in 3×3 and 2×4.

        P. Horodecki 1997

      • Unextendible product bases build bound entangled states Established

        A set of orthogonal product vectors with no further product vector orthogonal to it leaves a complement containing no product states. The normalised projector onto that complement is bound entangled.

        C. H. Bennett 1998

      • Low-rank PPT states are separable Established

        For an M×N state with M ≤ N and rank at most N, PPT is necessary and sufficient for separability, so there are no rank-3 PPT entangled states.

        P. Horodecki 2000 · B. Kraus 1999

      • Edge states sit at the boundary of PPT entanglement Established

        An edge state is a PPT entangled state from which no product vector can be subtracted while staying PPT. Lewenstein and coauthors use them to build optimal witnesses.

        M. Lewenstein 2000

      • Two-qutrit rank-four PPT entangled states all come from UPBs Established

        Every two-qutrit PPT entangled state of rank four can be built from an unextendible product basis. Chen and Đoković also determine the closure of this set.

        L. Chen 2011 · L. Chen 2012

    • Extendibility, and the limits of efficient testing Topic

      Entanglement cannot be freely shared. Asking how widely a state can be extended yields a complete test, along with a measure of how hard the problem is.

      A. C. Doherty 2003

      • A hierarchy of symmetric extensions detects all entanglement Established

        Separable states have symmetric extensions at every level. An entangled state fails at some finite level, and each level is a semidefinite program.

        A. C. Doherty 2003

      • Monogamy limits how far correlations can be shared Established

        Koashi and Winter give an identity trading entanglement with one party against classical correlation with another, from which rigorous monogamy relations follow.

        M. Koashi 2003

      • No efficient complete test is expected Established

        Deciding separability is NP-hard, even within an inverse polynomial distance of the separable set. Any complete classification by easy tests is therefore not expected unless P = NP.

        L. Gurvits 2003 · S. Gharibian 2008

    • Moments of the partial transpose can be measured directly Established

      Randomised local measurements estimate moments of the partial transpose without full tomography. Yu, Imai and Gühne find the optimal entanglement certification these moments allow.

      A. Elben 2020 · X.-D. Yu 2021

  • Free and bound entanglement Topic

    Can a state be turned back into pure entangled pairs? This operational question divides entangled states into free and bound, and leaves one major boundary unsettled.

    M. Horodecki 1998 · B. C. Hiesmayr 2024

    • Distillable states can be purified into singlets Established

      Given many sufficiently pure noisy pairs, LOCC can extract fewer pairs of arbitrarily high fidelity. This defines the free entangled class.

      C. H. Bennett 1995 · C. H. Bennett 1996

    • Every entangled two-qubit state is distillable Established

      In the smallest case, the classes coincide: entangled and free mean the same thing.

      M. Horodecki 1996

    • PPT entangled states cannot be distilled Established

      A distillable state must violate the partial transpose test. PPT entangled states therefore form a class of bound entanglement.

      M. Horodecki 1998

    • Is every NPT state distillable? Topic

      Bound entanglement is known on the PPT side. Whether it also exists on the NPT side is the central open question of this classification.

      D. P. DiVincenzo 1999 · W. Dür 1999

      • The question reduces to Werner states Established

        Local filtering and twirling take any NPT state to an NPT Werner state. If all NPT Werner states are distillable, then so are all NPT states.

        D. P. DiVincenzo 1999 · W. Dür 1999

      • Do bound entangled states with a negative partial transpose exist? Open

        Every distillable state is NPT. Evidence going back to 1999 suggests the converse may fail in dimensions above 2×2, with Werner states as the key test family.

        Still unknown: The question remains listed among the five open problems of Horodecki, Rudnicki and Życzkowski, and was still called long-standing in 2024. A 2006 preprint claimed existence, but later work continues to treat the question as open.

        D. P. DiVincenzo 1999 · W. Dür 1999 · P. Horodecki 2020 · S.-Y. Qi 2024 · R. Simon 2006

      • NPT Werner states are two-copy undistillable in every dimension Claimed

        A July 2026 preprint proves a partial-trace matrix inequality and uses it to claim two-copy undistillability of NPT Werner states in arbitrary local dimension.

        Still unknown: No independent confirmation had been found by the check date. Even if correct, it covers two copies, while NPT bound entanglement requires every number of copies.

        Z. Song 2026

      • PPT-preserving channels distil every NPT state Established

        All NPT states are distillable under channels that preserve the partial transpose's positivity. The open question therefore turns on the gap between separable and PPT-preserving operations.

        T. Eggeling 2001

  • Sorting states by the correlations they support Topic

    Entanglement is one rung in a ladder of correlations, with discord below it and steering and Bell nonlocality above. The picture changes once local filtering, many copies or side resources are allowed.

    H. M. Wiseman 2006 · H. Ollivier 2001

    • Quantum discord separates classical from quantum correlation Established

      Discord compares two forms of mutual information that agree classically. Its vanishing is a stricter condition than separability, so some separable states still carry it.

      H. Ollivier 2001 · L. Henderson 2001

    • Steering lies strictly between entanglement and Bell nonlocality Established

      Wiseman, Jones and Doherty define steering operationally. Bell nonlocal states are steerable and steerable states are entangled, and neither inclusion can be reversed.

      H. M. Wiseman 2006

    • Can entangled states have a local model? Topic

      Entangled states need not violate a Bell inequality directly. What looks local can however become nonlocal once more resources are allowed.

      R. F. Werner 1989 · J. Barrett 2001

      • Some entangled states admit a local hidden-variable model Established

        Werner built a model for projective measurements on some entangled states. Barrett extended the conclusion to general POVM measurements.

        R. F. Werner 1989 · J. Barrett 2001

      • Local filtering can reveal hidden nonlocality Established

        Popescu showed that sequential measurements, with a local filter first, can reveal Bell nonlocality in states that have a local model for direct measurements.

        S. Popescu 1995

      • Copies of a local state can violate a Bell inequality Established

        Palazuelos showed that tensoring a local state with itself can produce a Bell violation, so nonlocality is not fixed by the behaviour of a single copy.

        C. Palazuelos 2012

      • All entangled states are nonlocal in semi-quantum games Established

        Buscemi shows that a suitable class of nonlocal games, in which referees ask quantum questions, witnesses every entangled state. So what counts as nonlocal depends on the test.

        F. Buscemi 2011

      • Asymptotic CHSH violation is equivalent to distillability Established

        If local processing over arbitrarily many copies is allowed, violating CHSH is the same as being distillable. Bound entangled states do not violate it in this asymptotic sense.

        Ll. Masanes 2005

    • Do nonlocality and bound entanglement exclude each other? Topic

      Peres conjectured that PPT states would admit local models. Both the original form and its steering-based strengthening were later disproved.

      T. Vértesi 2014 · T. Moroder 2014

      • Bound entangled states never violate a Bell inequality Refuted

        False. Vértesi and Brunner found a bound entangled state violating a Bell inequality, so nonlocality implies neither distillability nor a negative partial transpose.

        T. Vértesi 2014

      • Bound entangled states can never be used for steering Refuted

        False. Moroder and coauthors gave bound entangled states that show steering, a counterexample to the stronger Peres conjecture.

        T. Moroder 2014

    • Sorting states by what they can do Topic

      A task splits states into useful and useless ones. Teleportation and key distillation draw lines that differ from distillability, and from each other.

      P. Horodecki 1998 · K. Horodecki 2003

      • Teleportation fidelity is fixed by singlet fraction Established

        Best teleportation fidelity is directly tied to the maximal singlet fraction attainable by trace-preserving LOCC, giving a task-based threshold on states.

        P. Horodecki 1998

      • States useful for teleportation are nonlocal resources Established

        Cavalcanti, Acín, Brunner and Vértesi show every entangled state useful for teleportation yields a Bell violation in a suitable scheme, linking the two classes.

        D. Cavalcanti 2012

      • Every entangled state can enhance teleportation of some state Established

        Masanes proved that any bipartite entangled state, even a bound one, can raise the teleportation power of some other state.

        Ll. Masanes 2005

      • Bound entangled states can yield a secret key Established

        Horodecki, Horodecki, Horodecki and Oppenheim characterise states containing a private key and show key can be distilled from bound entangled states, so privacy draws a different line from distillability.

        K. Horodecki 2003

  • Classification depends on the allowed operations Topic

    Each class of free operations gives a different resource theory, with different equivalences and conversions. Enlarging the class often simplifies the mathematics, while raising the question of what physical meaning remains.

    E. Chitambar 2018 · E. Chitambar 2012

    • Entanglement is a resource theory of free states and operations Established

      Choosing the free states and free operations sets the rules, and comparing theories becomes a way to see which features depend on those rules.

      E. Chitambar 2018 · M. Horodecki 2012

    • LOCC is physically natural yet mathematically hard Established

      LOCC captures what separated labs can do, but its structure is complex enough that even characterising the class is difficult, which motivates the larger classes below.

      E. Chitambar 2012

    • Separable operations strictly contain LOCC Established

      Rains studied entanglement purification under separable superoperators. Nonlocality without entanglement shows orthogonal product states that LOCC cannot reliably distinguish, a task separable measurements manage.

      E. M. Rains 1997 · C. H. Bennett 1998

    • PPT-preserving operations make many quantities computable Topic

      Relaxing LOCC to operations that preserve the partial transpose's positivity turns many optimisations into semidefinite programs, and gives bounds for LOCC.

      E. M. Rains 2000

      • PPT distillation fidelity is a semidefinite program Established

        Rains shows the maximum fidelity of a PPT distillation protocol solves a semidefinite program. This gives new upper bounds on LOCC-distillable entanglement.

        E. M. Rains 2000

      • PPT entanglement cost is tied to logarithmic negativity Established

        Audenaert, Plenio and Eisert relate PPT cost directly to log-negativity, and show that cost and distillation coincide for the antisymmetric Werner state.

        K. Audenaert 2002

      • Entanglement is irreversible under PPT operations Established

        Wang and Duan resolved this question with a computable additive bound on the regularised relative entropy of entanglement, showing a gap between cost and distillation for some states.

        X. Wang 2016

    • Under which operations is entanglement reversible? Topic

      A second law of entanglement would mean one quantity decides all conversions. Whether this holds depends sharply on the free operations.

      F. G. S. L. Brandão 2007 · L. Lami 2021

      • Entanglement manipulation under LOCC is irreversible Established

        Creating a bound entangled state costs entanglement, yet none can be recovered, so LOCC has no single entanglement currency for mixed states.

        G. Vidal 2001 · D. Yang 2005

      • Entanglement is reversible under all non-entangling operations Refuted

        False. Lami and Regula showed that entanglement theory stays irreversible even under all non-entangling transformations.

        L. Lami 2021

      • Reversibility under asymptotically non-entangling operations Established

        Once operations may generate vanishing entanglement, reversibility holds with the regularised relative entropy as the single quantity. The proof rests on the generalised quantum Stein's lemma, whose original proof had a gap repaired in 2024.

        F. G. S. L. Brandão 2007 · M. Berta 2022 · M. Hayashi 2024 · L. Lami 2024

    • The Choi correspondence reads states as channels Topic

      Bipartite states and channels are two views of the same object, so classifications of one carry over to the other.

      M.-D. Choi 1975 · M. Horodecki 2003

      • Separable Choi states correspond to entanglement-breaking channels Established

        A channel destroys all entanglement with a reference exactly when its Choi state is separable.

        M. Horodecki 2003

      • Is the composition of two PPT maps always entanglement breaking? Conjectured

        Equivalently, can entanglement swapping across two PPT links ever produce entanglement? The conjecture says it cannot.

        Still unknown: It has been proved in dimension 3 and for Gaussian channels. Higher finite dimensions remain open.

        M. Christandl 2018 · L. Chen 2018 · J. An 2026

  • Ordering states by quantity Topic

    Measures replace classes with numbers. For mixed states they split by construction, and they disagree about which state has more.

    R. Horodecki 2007

    • Measures are bracketed by distillation and formation Established

      A quantity qualifies as a measure by not increasing under LOCC. Any measure suited to the regime of many pairs lies between distillable entanglement and entanglement of formation.

      G. Vidal 1998 · M. Horodecki 1999

    • Different measures can disagree on which state is more entangled Established

      Eisert and Plenio compared measures directly. Virmani and Plenio showed that any two good asymptotic measures are either identical or order some states differently.

      J. Eisert 1998 · S. Virmani 1999

    • Measures built from pure-state decompositions Topic

      Extend a pure state measure to mixed states by taking the cheapest decomposition. These are physically direct but generally hard to compute.

      W. K. Wootters 1997

      • Entanglement of formation has a two-qubit closed form Established

        Wootters, building on Hill and Wootters, gave an explicit formula for any two-qubit state. Beyond this, computing it generally needs optimisation.

        W. K. Wootters 1997 · S. Hill 1997

      • Concurrence extends to arbitrary dimensions Established

        Introduced for two qubits by Hill and Wootters, concurrence was generalised to higher dimensions through universal state inversion by Rungta and coauthors.

        S. Hill 1997 · P. Rungta 2001

      • Geometric measure: overlap with the nearest product state Established

        On pure states this measure uses the largest overlap with a product state, and extends to mixed states by a convex roof. Barnum and Linden studied related monotones.

        T.-C. Wei 2003 · H. Barnum 2001

      • Entanglement of formation is additive Refuted

        False. Shor tied this to other additivity questions, and Hastings's counterexample settled all of them, so one-copy formation does not give the cost.

        P. W. Shor 2003 · M. B. Hastings 2008

      • Entanglement cost is regularised entanglement of formation Established

        The asymptotic preparation cost equals the many-copy limit of formation, which is exact but hard to evaluate.

        P. M. Hayden 2000

    • Measures of distance to the separable set Topic

      Measure how far a state lies from separable states, or how much noise it takes to reach them.

      V. Vedral 1997 · G. Vidal 1998

      • Relative entropy measures distinguishability from separable states Established

        Vedral and coauthors proposed distance-based measures, of which relative entropy is central. Its regularisation is the quantity in the generalised Stein's lemma.

        V. Vedral 1997 · V. Vedral 1997

      • Relative entropy of entanglement is additive Refuted

        False. Vollbrecht and Werner gave a counterexample, which is why asymptotic theorems use the regularised version.

        K. G. H. Vollbrecht 2000

      • Robustness measures the noise needed to remove entanglement Established

        Vidal and Tarrach define it as the minimal mixing with locally prepared states that washes out all entanglement.

        G. Vidal 1998

      • Smoothed distance measures give one-shot rates Established

        Brandão and Datta derive one-shot conversion rates under non-entangling maps from a smoothed max-relative entropy, and Buscemi and Datta study entanglement cost in a similar setting.

        F. G. S. L. Brandao 2009 · F. Buscemi 2009

    • Measures defined by tasks or by what can be calculated Topic

      Some measures come from a task, while others are chosen because they can be evaluated or have useful properties. Rarely is a measure all three.

      R. Horodecki 2007

      • Negativity and log-negativity can be computed for any state Established

        These come directly from the partial transpose. Log-negativity is a monotone despite not being convex. Being zero on all PPT states, neither sees bound entanglement.

        G. Vidal 2001 · M. B. Plenio 2005

      • Squashed entanglement is additive and faithful Established

        Built from conditional mutual information minimised over extensions, it is additive and is strictly positive on all entangled states.

        M. Christandl 2003 · F. G. S. L. Brandão 2010

      • Can distillable entanglement be computed in general? Open

        The one-way rate has a coding theorem, but it is a regularised optimisation over many copies.

        Still unknown: Single-letter formulas are known only in special classes, with new non-degradable families added in 2026. No general tractable formula is known.

        I. Devetak 2003 · F. Leditzky 2017 · R. G. Ahmed 2026

  • Where the classification can be completed Topic

    Structure and symmetry make some families fully solvable. They are the test beds for every general conjecture on this page.

    R. Horodecki 2007

    • Two qubits are classified completely Established

      PPT decides separability, every entangled state is distillable, and formation has a closed form. Here the main classifications agree.

      M. Horodecki 1996 · M. Horodecki 1996 · W. K. Wootters 1997

    • Twirling reduces states to one-parameter symmetric families Established

      Averaging over U⊗U gives Werner states and over U⊗U* gives isotropic states, each with one parameter. Their symmetry makes measures and distillation calculations far simpler.

      R. F. Werner 1989 · K. G. H. Vollbrecht 2000 · M. Horodecki 1997

    • Bipartite stabilizer states reduce to EPR pairs Established

      Local unitaries reduce any bipartite stabilizer state to EPR pairs and local states, so the pair count fully sets its entanglement.

      D. Fattal 2004 · S. Bravyi 2005

    • Gaussian states are classified by covariance matrices Topic

      For Gaussian states of continuous-variable systems, separability and distillability reduce to conditions on a finite covariance matrix.

      R. Simon 1999 · R. F. Werner 2000

      • PPT decides separability for 1×N mode Gaussian states Established

        Simon and Duan and coauthors gave two-mode criteria. Werner and Wolf extended the PPT result to one mode against any number of modes.

        R. Simon 1999 · L.-M. Duan 1999 · R. F. Werner 2000

      • Bound entangled Gaussian states exist in 2×2 modes Established

        With two modes on each side, Werner and Wolf give a family of explicit counterexamples: PPT no longer implies separable.

        R. F. Werner 2000

      • A computable criterion decides separability for all Gaussian states Established

        Giedke and coauthors give a necessary and sufficient, operational test for any number of modes. It is independent of partial transposition and strictly stronger.

        G. Giedke 2001

      • All inseparable two-mode Gaussian states are distillable Established

        Giedke, Duan, Cirac and Zoller show that every entangled Gaussian state of two infinite-dimensional systems can be distilled. Their protocol includes a non-Gaussian step, a measurement of total particle number.

        G. Giedke 2000

      • Gaussian operations alone cannot distil Gaussian states Established

        Giedke and Cirac characterise Gaussian operations and show they cannot distil Gaussian states. Eisert, Scheel and Plenio rule out the corresponding two-copy iterative protocols, so distillation needs a non-Gaussian step.

        G. Giedke 2002 · J. Eisert 2002

  • Sorting many-body states by how entanglement grows Topic

    For a large system, the class of a state is how entanglement across a cut grows with the region. This classifies states by physics and by how hard they are to simulate.

    J. Eisert 2008

    • Gapped 1D ground states obey an area law Established

      Hastings proved that ground states of gapped one-dimensional Hamiltonians have block entropy bounded by a constant, independent of block size.

      M. B. Hastings 2007 · J. Eisert 2008

    • Do gapped 2D ground states obey an area law? Partial

      Anshu, Arad and Gosset prove an area law for locally gapped frustration-free 2D spin systems, with respect to a vertical bipartition of the lattice.

      Still unknown: The result does not yet cover general gapped two-dimensional ground states or arbitrary regions.

      A. Anshu 2021 · J. Eisert 2008

    • Critical 1D systems have logarithmic corrections Established

      Calabrese and Cardy show that entanglement entropy in 1+1-dimensional conformal field theories grows logarithmically with interval length, with a coefficient set by the central charge.

      P. Calabrese 2004

    • Random pure states are nearly maximally entangled Established

      Page computed the average subsystem entropy of a random pure state, which is close to maximal. Hayden, Leung and Winter show this concentrates sharply, and that large subspaces contain only almost maximally entangled states.

      D. N. Page 1993 · P. Hayden 2004

    • A constant correction detects topological order Established

      Kitaev and Preskill, and Levin and Wen, independently extract a universal constant from the entropy of a region, which detects topological order in a ground state.

      A. Kitaev 2005 · M. Levin 2005

    • The entanglement spectrum holds more than entropy Established

      Li and Haldane propose the full spectrum of the reduced state as a generalisation of entropy, one that can identify topological order.

      H. Li 2008

    • Limited entanglement allows efficient classical simulation Established

      Vidal shows that low Schmidt rank across cuts allows efficient simulation. Schuch and coauthors show even a von Neumann area law is not enough for matrix product state approximation, so Rényi entropies matter.

      G. Vidal 2003 · N. Schuch 2007
