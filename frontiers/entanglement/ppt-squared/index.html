Entanglement map · Conjectured

Is the composition of two PPT maps always entanglement breaking?

Widely expected, not proved in general.

Equivalently: can two PPT entangled links ever generate entanglement between their ends through entanglement swapping? The conjecture says no.

Still unknown

It has been proved in dimension 3 and for Gaussian channels. Higher finite dimensions remain open.
