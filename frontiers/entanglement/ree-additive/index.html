Entanglement map · Refuted

Relative entropy of entanglement is additive

The statement shown is false.

The relative entropy of entanglement measures distance to the separable set. Vollbrecht and Werner found a counterexample to its additivity, which is why the regularised version is the quantity that appears in asymptotic theorems.
