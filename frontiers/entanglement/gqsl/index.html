The generalised quantum Stein's lemma, and reversibility under asymptotically non-entangling operations

Proved, with a source.

The Stein exponent for testing an entangled state against separable states equals the regularised relative entropy of entanglement. This gives reversibility when operations may generate vanishing entanglement. The original 2010 proof had a gap, found in 2022. Hayashi and Yamasaki, and Lami, then gave independent proofs in 2024, and the Hayashi–Yamasaki proof has been formalised in Lean.
