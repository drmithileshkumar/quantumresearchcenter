Do bound entangled states with a negative partial transpose exist?

Unresolved.

Every distillable state is NPT. Is the converse true? In dimensions above 2×2, evidence going back to 1999 suggests some NPT states may be undistillable, with Werner states the central family under study.

Still unknown

It remains listed among the five open problems of Horodecki, Rudnicki and Życzkowski, and a 2024 paper still calls it long-standing. A 2006 preprint claimed existence, but later work continues to treat the question as open.
