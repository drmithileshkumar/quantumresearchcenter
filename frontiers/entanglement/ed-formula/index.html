Can distillable entanglement be computed in general?

Unresolved.

The one-way rate has a coding theorem, but it is a regularised optimisation over many copies.

Still unknown

Single-letter formulas are known only in special classes, such as degradable and PPT states, with new non-degradable families added in 2026. No general tractable formula is known, so most work goes into bounds.
