What would it mean for reduction to be an actual physical process?

7.1 Changing the dynamics

7.1.1 Specify a jump law and its probability density

Objective-collapse models change quantum dynamics so that macroscopic alternatives do not indefinitely coexist as one uncollapsed superposition. They are therefore more than different verbal readings of an unchanged formalism. A complete model must specify when localization occurs, how the state changes, and how outcomes are distributed.

In a simple GRW-type position-jump model for distinguishable particles, each particle undergoes localization events at rate λ. A jump for particle i with center x acts through

\[L_i(x)=(\pi r_C^2)^{-3/4}\exp[-(\hat q_i-x)^2/(2r_C^2)],\qquad \int d^3x\,L_i(x)^\dagger L_i(x)=I.\]

The center has probability density \(\|L_i(x)\Psi\|^2\), and the updated vector is \(L_i(x)\Psi/\|L_i(x)\Psi\|\). Between jumps the state evolves with the Hamiltonian. Here r_C fixes the Gaussian convention; other texts can parameterize the same width differently.

The jump multiplies the configuration wavefunction by a localized Gaussian. It narrows spatial alternatives probabilistically, rather than asking an observer to declare a measurement. Gaussian tails remain nonzero, however, so the interpretation of macroscopic definiteness and the choice of material ontology still need attention. A wavefunction law alone is not a complete description of flashes or mass density.

Worked check 7.1. For Nλt=3, find the probability of at least one jump.

Reasoned solution

It is 1−e⁻³≈0.9502. This is an event probability, not a universal branch-resolution probability.

7.2 Amplification and ensemble evolution

7.2.1 Separate trajectories from their average

If N distinguishable constituents each have independent rate λ, the probability of no event over time t is exp(−Nλt). For a rigid superposition whose corresponding particle locations are separated far beyond r_C, one localization can strongly suppress one branch relative to the other. This is the basic amplification mechanism. The formula does not claim that every event resolves every possible pair of states: separations and geometry matter.

Averaging the jump process gives an ensemble master equation of the form

\[\dot\rho=-\frac{i}{\hbar}[H,\rho]+\lambda\sum_i\left(\int d^3x\,L_i(x)\rho L_i(x)^\dagger-\rho\right).\]

The jump law supplies more information than this average. To see why, consider the toy qubit equation \(\dot\rho=\kappa(Z\rho Z-\rho)\). Its off-diagonal entries decay at rate 2κ. Random unitary Z flips generate this same equation, yet an individual |+⟩ trajectory then merely switches between |+⟩ and |−⟩. It does not localize into a Z eigenstate.

Thus decay of an ensemble coherence is not enough to establish collapse of individual states. The state-vector stochastic process, its ontology, and the ensemble predictions must be specified separately. The qubit example is a warning about inference, not a replacement for the spatial collapse model.

Worked check 7.2. Solve the toy master equation for ρ₀₁.

Reasoned solution

Since (ZρZ)₀₁=−ρ₀₁, its derivative is −2κρ₀₁ and ρ₀₁(t)=e⁻²κᵗρ₀₁(0).

7.3 A testable proposal

7.3.1 Distinguish new dynamics from ordinary environmental noise

A modified theory can be investigated experimentally when its additional dynamics predicts observable differences. In collapse models, suppressed interference, momentum diffusion, or heating can provide signatures, depending on the model and coupling. Ordinary gas collisions, radiation, vibration, and detector noise can also suppress interference. A credible test must compare quantitative models rather than treating any missing visibility as evidence for collapse.

For an explicitly simplified visibility model, write \(V(t)=V_0\exp[-(\Gamma_{\rm env}+\Gamma_{\rm new})t]\). If environmental decay is independently known and the measured visibility has a lower bound V_min, the allowed extra rate satisfies

\[\Gamma_{\rm new}\le-\frac1t\log(V_{\min}/V_0)-\Gamma_{\rm env}.\]

This bound is conditional on the exponential model, calibrated visibility, and uncertainty treatment. A negative right side signals incompatibility of those inputs with a nonnegative additional rate; it is not a measured negative collapse rate. Real constraints on λ and r_C require the model-specific relationship between localization parameters and the measured signal.

The research habit here is transferable: publish assumptions, units, nuisance parameters, and statistical confidence. Distinguish a bound on one parameterized collapse model from a verdict about all interpretations. No current experimental limit is asserted by our hypothetical examples.

Worked check 7.3. With V₀=1, V_min=0.9, t=1 second, and Γ_env=0.05 s⁻¹, bound Γ_new.

Reasoned solution

The hypothetical bound is −ln(0.9)−0.05≈0.05536 s⁻¹, assuming the stated environmental estimate and visibility model.
