20.1 The question and the apparatus

Imagine a microscopic trigger coupled to a detector and then to a macroscopic record. Schrödinger's cat makes the consequences vivid by replacing a pointer with two macroscopically distinct cat conditions. No animal experiment is needed: the thought experiment asks what follows if the same linear dynamics describes the trigger, detector, and record.

A reliable interaction maps |0⟩|ready⟩ to |0⟩|record 0⟩ and |1⟩|ready⟩ to |1⟩|record 1⟩. Linearity then maps a superposition of inputs into a superposition of correlated records. The interaction explains why each term contains a consistent record. It does not, by itself, select one term as the sole outcome.

The visual uses two record boxes rather than a literal animated animal. Their weights are branch probabilities in the model, not measured percentages of a partly living organism. A coherence control compares the full coherent description with the reduced statistics available after environmental information is ignored.

20.2 Build a prediction

For a balanced trigger, the joint pure state is \(|\Psi\rangle=(|0,R_0\rangle+|1,R_1\rangle)/\sqrt2\). An incoherent mixture assigns probability 1/2 to each product state. Both predict the same pointer-basis counts, but they differ on suitable joint interference measurements.

In the two-dimensional subspace spanned by |0,R0⟩ and |1,R1⟩, write ρ=½[[1,γ],[γ*,1]]. The pure balanced state has γ=1 and the fully dephased mixture has γ=0. Measuring in the joint ± basis gives P+=(1+Reγ)/2. Thus equal record frequencies do not imply identical global states.

If the environment stores states |E0⟩ and |E1⟩, the accessible coherence is multiplied by ⟨E1|E0⟩. Nearly orthogonal environment states suppress interference in the reduced description. The formal operation of tracing out the environment is not an additional law that chooses one unique global result.

20.3 What the evidence can establish

The measurement problem concerns how to connect the formal description to definite experienced outcomes. Interpretations and modified dynamics address it differently. Decoherence explains why interference between certain records becomes locally inaccessible and why stable pointer descriptions are useful, but it does not alone select an interpretation.

Laboratory cat states are coherent superpositions of distinguishable states in controlled systems, such as field modes or collective degrees of freedom. Calling them cats does not mean that a living animal's conscious state has been coherently controlled. Assess the actual Hilbert space, separation, and coherence witness used in a claim.

The diagram's branches are a bookkeeping tool shared by the calculation. It neither declares multiple worlds real nor assumes an objective collapse. Read it with an assumption ledger: linear evolution, the meaning of a state, and the account of outcomes are separate commitments.

Explore the experiment

Reduce coherence while watching the record probabilities remain 1/2 each. Compare the joint + measurement, which changes, with the pointer counts, which do not.

A worked example

A reduced record subspace has real γ=0.4. What is P+ and how does it compare with pointer statistics?

P+=(1+0.4)/2=0.7. The two pointer outcomes remain 0.5 each. Interference measurements distinguish states that pointer counts alone cannot.

Try it yourself

Problem 20.1

Does a diagonal reduced state prove a global collapse happened?

Hint

Compare a partial trace with a dynamical change.

Worked solution

No. Entanglement with an ignored environment can give the same local diagonal matrix while the global state remains pure.

Problem 20.2

What is the difference between a coherent state sum and a classical mixture here?

Hint

Inspect the off-diagonal terms.

Worked solution

The coherent sum includes cross terms that affect joint interference. The mixture lacks those terms even though pointer frequencies agree.

Problem 20.3

Why should experimental cat claims specify the degrees of freedom?

Hint

The metaphor covers very different physical systems.

Worked solution

The preparation and coherence witness apply to a particular system, such as two oscillator wave packets. They do not automatically establish coherent control over all macroscopic or biological variables.

What to carry forward

Correlation, loss of accessible interference, and a unique outcome are different explanatory tasks. The cat makes their separation explicit.
