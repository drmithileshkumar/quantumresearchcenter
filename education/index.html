The courses

10 courses

Basics

5 courses
  • 01

    Quantum Computing

    Build from classical computation to quantum states, circuits, and algorithms.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Basic linear algebra. No prior quantum mechanics required.

  • 02

    Quantum Algorithms

    Explore the techniques behind interference, quantum search, and simulation.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Computing course or equivalent knowledge.

  • 03

    Quantum Programming

    Turn quantum algorithms into testable, reproducible programs with Qiskit.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Computing and Quantum Algorithms, or equivalent knowledge. Python essentials are introduced in the course.

  • 04

    Quantum Information

    Study quantum states, entanglement, and the limits of communication.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    No prior quantum mechanics, information theory, or linear algebra course required. Mathematics is developed in context.

  • 05

    Quantum Hardware

    Connect qubits and gates to physical devices and quantum processors.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Computing course or equivalent knowledge. Device physics is introduced in context.

Advanced

5 courses
  • 06

    Quantum Foundations

    Derive quantum theory from physical principles and examine what its assumptions mean.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Comfort with vectors, matrices, and probability at the level of a first university course. No prior quantum mechanics required.

  • 07

    Quantum Information Theory

    Derive operational bounds and study the limits of quantum communication and resources.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Foundations and introductory Quantum Information, or equivalent knowledge of density operators, tensor products, measurements, and entropy. Comfort with linear algebra and probability.

  • 08

    Quantum Error Correction

    Construct quantum codes and carry their protection through noisy computations.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Information Theory and basic Quantum Computing, or equivalent knowledge of states, channels, Pauli operators, reference systems, and circuits. Binary coding theory is introduced in context.

  • 09

    Quantum Cryptography

    Turn quantum signals into secret keys and examine the assumptions behind security.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Information or equivalent knowledge of qubits, measurement, tensor products, and elementary probability. No prior cryptography required.

  • 10

    Interpretations of Quantum Mechanics

    Compare interpretations through shared experiments, explicit assumptions, and mathematical arguments.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Foundations or equivalent knowledge of states, density operators, tensor products, measurement, and probability. Quantum Information Theory is helpful; elementary calculus is used for Bohmian dynamics.

Self-paced textbooks by Mithilesh Kumar. Courses are being developed; each course describes its current scope.

The Basics courses cover quantum computing, algorithms, programming, information, and hardware. The advanced sequence begins with Quantum Foundations, continues with Quantum Information Theory, and develops reliable computation in Quantum Error Correction. Interpretations of Quantum Mechanics offers a parallel advanced path from Quantum Foundations into measurement, reality, and quantum observers. Open the prerequisites above to find the right fit.
