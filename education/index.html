    11 developed chapters · Worked examples and solved problems · By Mithilesh Kumar

    Prerequisite: Quantum Computing course or equivalent knowledge.

    Quantum Programming

    Turn quantum algorithms into correct, testable, and reproducible programs with Qiskit. Explore language design, circuit construction, simulation, compilation, and hardware execution.

    16 developed chapters · Worked examples and solved problems · By Mithilesh Kumar

    Prerequisite: Quantum Computing and Quantum Algorithms, or equivalent knowledge. Python essentials are introduced in the course.

    View course →

  • Self-paced course · Draft textbook

    Quantum Information

    From distinguishable messages and classical information to quantum states, entanglement, compression, error correction, communication, and cryptography. Develop the necessary mathematics through concrete information-processing tasks.

    16 developed chapters · Worked examples and solved problems · By Mithilesh Kumar

    Prerequisite: No prior quantum mechanics, information theory, or linear algebra course required. Mathematics is developed in context.

    View course →

  • Self-paced course · Draft textbook

    Quantum Hardware

    From physical qubits, control, coupling, and readout to hardware platforms, bosonic and topological encodings, processor architecture, and fault-tolerant implementation.

    19 developed chapters · Worked examples and solved problems · By Mithilesh Kumar

    Prerequisite: Quantum Computing course or equivalent knowledge. Device physics is introduced in context.
