The courses

05 courses
  • 01

    Quantum Computing

    Build from classical computation to quantum states, circuits, and algorithms.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Basic linear algebra. No prior quantum mechanics required.

  • 02

    Quantum Algorithms

    Explore the techniques behind interference, quantum search, and simulation.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Computing course or equivalent knowledge.

  • 03

    Quantum Programming

    Turn quantum algorithms into testable, reproducible programs with Qiskit.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Computing and Quantum Algorithms, or equivalent knowledge. Python essentials are introduced in the course.

  • 04

    Quantum Information

    Study quantum states, entanglement, and the limits of communication.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    No prior quantum mechanics, information theory, or linear algebra course required. Mathematics is developed in context.

  • 05

    Quantum Hardware

    Connect qubits and gates to physical devices and quantum processors.

    ↗
    Prerequisites

    Quantum Computing course or equivalent knowledge. Device physics is introduced in context.
