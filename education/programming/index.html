Quantum Programming

From circuit specifications to reproducible programs

Turn quantum computations into executable programs with Qiskit. Each chapter has a runnable companion, explicit mathematical predictions, and checks. The sequence progresses from language design and local simulation to compilation, dynamic circuits, noise, and a reproducible project.

Begin reading →Continue where you left off →

Prerequisites and approach

Quantum Computing and Quantum Algorithms, or equivalent knowledge. Python concepts are introduced through the examples.

16 of 16 chapters contain draft lessons with worked examples and solved problems. These are teaching manuscripts in development; advanced results are stated with their assumptions and sources where a full proof lies beyond the course.
