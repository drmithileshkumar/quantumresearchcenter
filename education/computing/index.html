Quantum Computing

From classical to quantum

What changes when computation follows the rules of quantum mechanics? This book builds the answer from the ground up: from mechanical reasoning and classical machines to quantum states, circuits, and algorithms.

Begin reading →Continue where you left off →

Prerequisites and approach

The exposition begins with motivation, develops a mathematical language, and connects it to examples. You do not need prior experience with models of computation. Basic linear algebra is the only subject prerequisite: vectors, matrices, bases, and linear transformations. Complex inner products, tensor products, discrete probability, and computational concepts are developed where they are needed.

Circuit drawings show how operations are assembled. Interactive figures let you prepare a Bell state, compare phase oracles, and watch how search redistributes amplitudes. Try predicting a result before moving a control, then explain what changed. Animations wait for you to start them.

Contents

  1. 01

    Logic and Computability

    The expression \(n^2+n+41\) gives \(41\), \(43\), and \(47\) when \(n\) is zero, one, and two. All three results are prime: their only positive divisors are one and themselves. It is tempting to wonder whether the expression always produces a prime. How much confidence should a few successful calculations give us?

    →
  2. 02

    Boolean and Reversible Computation

    A Boolean function specifies an answer, but its implementation may discard the information needed to reconstruct the input. We derive circuits from functions, then construct computations that preserve enough information to be reversed.

    →
  3. 03

    Probability Amplitudes and Interference

    The same single-step probabilities can conceal different behavior under composition. A two-state example shows why quantum predictions retain amplitudes, and why alternatives must be combined before outcome probabilities are calculated.

    →
  4. 04

    Quantum States and Measurement

    Basic linear algebra supplies the language, but physical postulates give that language meaning. We connect vectors, projections, and operators to preparations, transformations, and measurable outcomes.

    →
  5. 05

    Composite Systems and Entanglement

    Joint systems require joint amplitudes. Tensor products organize their coordinates; entanglement reveals why the state of the whole need not be a pair of individual state vectors.

    →
  6. 06

    Quantum Queries and Phase Kickback

    A quantum query can evaluate a function coherently, but readout remains restricted. A small decision problem shows how to encode a useful property in phase and turn it into a certain measurement result.

    →
  7. 07

    Quantum Search and Amplitude Amplification

    A phase mark does not itself make a useful answer more likely. We first solve a four-item search completely, then uncover the rotation that generalizes the calculation.

    →
  8. 08

    Quantum Circuits and Universality

    The preceding algorithms specify mathematical operations. Circuits expose how these operations are assembled and which implementation costs an abstract oracle can hide.

    →
  9. 09

    Computational Complexity and Quantum Advantage

    An advantage is meaningful only with a stated task, model, success condition, and resource. We distinguish algorithmic upper bounds, general lower bounds, and unresolved comparisons.

    →
  10. A–E

    Appendices

    These short extensions supply supporting models, identities, and formal arguments. Each can be read when its linked chapter raises the corresponding question.

    →

How to use this book

Read the chapters in order for a continuous path through the subject, or use the expandable contents to revisit a topic. Search works throughout the book. The text-size controls adjust the reading view, and each section has a permanent link.

Diagrams, equations, and interactive experiments are included with the book. Use your browser’s print command for a reading copy; interactive figures print their current state.
