Research papers and reviews

  1. Quantum Computing in the NISQ era and beyond

    John Preskill. 2018. Perspective

    Frames the relationship between noisy processors, useful computation, and fault tolerance.

  2. Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction

    Daniel Gottesman. 1997. Doctoral thesis

    Develops the stabilizer framework used to describe many quantum error-correcting codes.

  3. Quantum Simulation

    I. M. Georgescu, S. Ashhab, Franco Nori. 2013. Review

    Surveys using controllable quantum systems to study other quantum systems.

  4. Variational Quantum Algorithms

    M. Cerezo et al.. 2020. Review

    Introduces hybrid optimization and discusses its trainability, accuracy, and efficiency challenges.

  5. High-threshold and low-overhead fault-tolerant quantum memory

    Sergey Bravyi et al.. 2023. Research paper

    Studies a low-overhead memory protocol with explicit code, connectivity, decoding, and noise assumptions.

  6. Quantum error correction below the surface code threshold

    Rajeev Acharya et al.. 2024. Experimental research

    Reports surface-code memories with improving logical performance as code distance increases in the tested regime.

  7. Quantum error correction at ultra-low overhead

    Zhide Lu, Weikang Li, Dong-Ling Deng. 2026. Preprint / numerical study

    Proposes a high-rate code family with a hardware-oriented syndrome schedule; its reported performance depends on the simulated noise and architecture assumptions.

  8. t|ket⟩: A Retargetable Compiler for NISQ Devices

    Seyon Sivarajah et al.. 2020. Research paper

    Describes a compiler that translates and optimizes circuits for different target devices.

  9. A Verified Optimizer for Quantum Circuits

    Kesha Hietala et al.. 2019. Research paper

    Presents circuit transformations with machine-checked correctness proofs.

  10. Optimality Study of Existing Quantum Computing Layout Synthesis Tools

    Bochen Tan, Jason Cong. 2020. Research paper

    Uses benchmarks with known optima to examine qubit mapping and routing quality.

  11. Quantum entanglement

    Ryszard Horodecki et al.. 2007. Review

    A starting point for entanglement detection, quantification, and its use as a resource.

  12. Quantum repeaters: From quantum networks to the quantum internet

    Koji Azuma et al.. 2022. Review

    Connects repeater architectures with the challenge of distributing quantum information over long distances.

  13. Advances in Quantum Teleportation

    Stefano Pirandola et al.. 2015. Review

    Surveys the transfer of quantum states using shared entanglement and classical communication.

  14. The quantum capacity of channels with arbitrarily correlated noise

    Francesco Buscemi, Nilanjana Datta. 2009. Research paper

    Studies bounds on quantum communication rates in settings that include correlated noise.

  15. Quantum Non-Markovianity: Characterization, Quantification and Detection

    Ángel Rivas, Susana F. Huelga, Martin B. Plenio. 2014. Review

    Compares ways to define and detect memory in open quantum dynamics.

  16. Decoherence, the measurement problem, and interpretations of quantum mechanics

    Maximilian Schlosshauer. 2003. Review

    Clarifies the relationship between environmental decoherence and questions about measurement and interpretation.

  17. Bell nonlocality

    Nicolas Brunner et al.. 2013. Review

    Introduces Bell correlations and their role in the foundations and applications of quantum information.

  18. Self-testing of quantum systems: a review

    Ivan Šupić, Joseph Bowles. 2019. Review

    Explains how observed correlations can constrain a system’s underlying state and measurements, under explicit assumptions.

  19. Operational Markov condition for quantum processes

    Felix A. Pollock et al.. 2018. Research paper

    Connects memory to interventions performed at multiple times in a quantum process.

  20. Quantum sensing

    C. L. Degen, F. Reinhard, P. Cappellaro. 2016. Review

    Introduces quantum sensing methods and how physical signals become measurable quantum dynamics.

  21. Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum metrology using quantum error correction

    Sisi Zhou et al.. 2017. Research paper

    Derives conditions for error-corrected sensing under a specified noise and control model.

  22. Optimality and Noise-Resilience of Critical Quantum Sensing

    Uesli Alushi et al.. 2024. Research paper

    Compares critical and passive sensing while accounting for dissipation and resource constraints.

  23. Measurement-Induced Phase Transitions in the Dynamics of Entanglement

    Brian Skinner, Jonathan Ruhman, Adam Nahum. 2018. Research paper

    Studies competing entangling dynamics and measurements in many-body models.

  24. Measurement-induced entanglement and teleportation on a noisy quantum processor

    Jesse C. Hoke et al.. 2023. Experimental research

    Investigates measurement-induced information phases on a superconducting processor.

  25. Robust randomized benchmarking of quantum processes

    Easwar Magesan, J. M. Gambetta, Joseph Emerson. 2010. Research paper

    Develops a protocol for estimating average gate errors with stated assumptions about noise.
