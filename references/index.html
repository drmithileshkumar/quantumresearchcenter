Bibliography

Papers cited in the research handbook.

Research papers and reviews

  1. Decoherence, the measurement problem, and interpretations of quantum mechanics

    Maximilian Schlosshauer. 2003. Review

    Clarifies the relationship between environmental decoherence and questions about measurement and interpretation.

  2. Quantum Decoherence

    Maximilian Schlosshauer. 2019. Review

    Models, experiments, and the limits of decoherence as a foundational explanation.

  3. Negative entropy and information in quantum mechanics

    N. J. Cerf, C. Adami. 1995. Theory

    Relates negative conditional entropy to entanglement.

  4. Quantum information can be negative

    Michał Horodecki, Jonathan Oppenheim, Andreas Winter. 2005. Theory

    Gives conditional entropy an operational meaning as partial quantum information.

  5. Virtual quantum subsystems

    Paolo Zanardi. 2001. Theory

    Argues that accessible observables select the partition into subsystems.

  6. Quantum tensor product structures are observable-induced

    Paolo Zanardi, Daniel Lidar, Seth Lloyd. 2003. Theory

    Develops an algebraic framework in which the tensor product structure is relative to observables.

  7. Decoherence, einselection, and the quantum origins of the classical

    Wojciech H. Zurek. 2001. Review

    Reviews how interaction with an environment selects stable pointer states.

  8. Quantum Darwinism

    Wojciech H. Zurek. 2009. Research paper

    Redundant environmental records and the emergence of classical accessibility.

  9. Everett and structure

    David Wallace. 2001. Theory

    Treats the macroworld in the Everett interpretation as emergent structures and patterns.

  10. Relational quantum mechanics

    Carlo Rovelli. 1996. Theory

    Rejects the notion of an observer-independent state.

  11. Probabilities from entanglement, Born's rule from envariance

    W. H. Zurek. 2004. Theory

    Derives Born's rule for outcomes associated with Schmidt states from environment-assisted invariance.

  12. On Zurek's derivation of the Born rule

    Maximilian Schlosshauer, Arthur Fine. 2003. Theory

    Identifies assumptions entering the envariance derivation.

  13. Quantum theory cannot consistently describe the use of itself

    Daniela Frauchiger, Renato Renner. 2016. Theory

    Proposes a thought experiment with agents who apply quantum theory to each other.

  14. A strong no-go theorem on the Wigner's friend paradox

    Kok-Wei Bong et al.. 2019. Theory and experiment

    Proves that observer-scale quantum control conflicts with one of three assumptions, including absoluteness of observed events.

  15. "Relative state" formulation of quantum mechanics

    Hugh Everett III. 1957. Theory

    Introduces the state of a subsystem relative to a state of the remainder.

  16. Zur Theorie der linearen und nichtlinearen Integralgleichungen

    Erhard Schmidt. 1907. Theory

    The mathematical origin of the decomposition now named after Schmidt.

  17. Entangled quantum systems and the Schmidt decomposition

    Artur Ekert, Peter L. Knight. 1995. Theory

    A pedagogical account of the Schmidt decomposition for entangled systems.

  18. Discussion of probability relations between separated systems

    Erwin Schrödinger. 1935. Theory

    Introduces steering of one system through measurements on another.

  19. A complete classification of quantum ensembles having a given density matrix

    Lane P. Hughston, Richard Jozsa, William K. Wootters. 1993. Theory

    Classifies the pure-state ensembles of a density matrix.

  20. Triorthogonal uniqueness theorem and its relevance to the interpretation of quantum mechanics

    Andrew Elby, Jeffrey Bub. 1994. Theory

    Shows that a three-way orthogonal decomposition is unique when it exists.

  21. Pointer basis of quantum apparatus: into what mixture does the wave packet collapse?

    Wojciech H. Zurek. 1981. Theory

    Ties the apparatus's preferred basis to its interaction with the environment.

  22. Modal interpretations of quantum mechanics

    Olimpia Lombardi, Dennis Dieks. 2012. Review

    Surveys modal interpretations, including the role of the biorthogonal decomposition and its difficulties.

  23. Quantum steering ellipsoids

    Sania Jevtic, Matthew F. Pusey, David Jennings, Terry Rudolph. 2013. Theory

    Represents a two-qubit state by the set of Bloch vectors one party can steer the other to.

  24. Bell nonlocality

    Nicolas Brunner et al.. 2013. Review

    Introduces Bell correlations and their role in the foundations and applications of quantum information.

  25. Information Causality as a Physical Principle

    Marcin Pawłowski et al.. 2009. Research paper

    An operational restriction on accessible information beyond no-signalling.

  26. Quantum Darwinism in a hazy environment

    Michael Zwolak, H. T. Quan, Wojciech H. Zurek. 2009. Research paper

    The effect of initial environmental entropy on record storage.

  27. Gravitationally-induced entanglement between two massive particles is sufficient evidence of quantum effects in gravity

    Chiara Marletto, Vlatko Vedral. 2017. Research proposal

    A gravity-mediated entanglement proposal; interpret the conclusion with its mediator assumptions.

  28. What gravity mediated entanglement can really tell us about quantum gravity

    Eduardo Martín-Martínez, T. Rick Perche. 2022. Research paper

    Examines the locality and modelling assumptions behind gravity-mediated entanglement tests.

  29. Classical theories of gravity produce entanglement

    Joseph Aziz, Richard Howl. 2025. Research paper

    A proposed field-theoretic mechanism; its entanglement inference is disputed in subsequent work.

  30. Can classical theories of gravity produce entanglement?

    Anirudh Gundhi, Giorgia Infantino, Angelo Bassi. 2026. Preprint

    Challenges the amplitude treatment in the Aziz–Howl proposal; read alongside the original claim.

  31. Quantum Computational Complexity

    John Watrous. 2008. Survey

    Definitions and results for quantum decision, verification, and interactive proof classes.

  32. Polynomial-Time Algorithms for Prime Factorization and Discrete Logarithms on a Quantum Computer

    Peter W. Shor. 1995. Research paper

    Polynomial-time quantum algorithms; does not establish corresponding classical lower bounds.

  33. Strengths and Weaknesses of Quantum Computing

    Charles H. Bennett, Ethan Bernstein, Gilles Brassard, Umesh Vazirani. 1997. Research paper

    Black-box lower bounds and the limitations of unstructured quantum search.

  34. Quantum Simulation

    I. M. Georgescu, S. Ashhab, Franco Nori. 2013. Review

    Surveys using controllable quantum systems to study other quantum systems.

  35. A Theory of Trotter Error

    Andrew M. Childs, Yuan Su, Minh C. Tran, Nathan Wiebe, Shuchen Zhu. 2019. Research paper

    Product-formula simulation errors controlled by commutator structure.

  36. Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction

    Daniel Gottesman. 1997. Doctoral thesis

    Develops the stabilizer framework used to describe many quantum error-correcting codes.

  37. Concatenated Quantum Codes

    Emanuel Knill, Raymond Laflamme. 1996. Research paper

    A construction supporting fault-tolerant scaling under specified error assumptions.

  38. Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computation with Constant Overhead

    Daniel Gottesman. 2013. Research paper

    Asymptotic constant qubit-overhead constructions with stated code and fault-tolerance requirements.

  39. High-threshold and low-overhead fault-tolerant quantum memory

    Sergey Bravyi et al.. 2023. Research paper

    Studies a low-overhead memory protocol with explicit code, connectivity, decoding, and noise assumptions.

  40. Quantum entanglement

    Ryszard Horodecki et al.. 2007. Review

    A starting point for entanglement detection, quantification, and its use as a resource.

  41. Mixed State Entanglement and Quantum Error Correction

    Charles H. Bennett, David P. DiVincenzo, John A. Smolin, William K. Wootters. 1996. Research paper

    Connects purification of shared entanglement with quantum error correction.

  42. Secure key from bound entanglement

    Karol Horodecki, Michał Horodecki, Paweł Horodecki, Jonathan Oppenheim. 2003. Research paper

    Shows that secret-key utility and distillable Bell-pair entanglement are different resources.

  43. Capacities of Quantum Erasure Channels

    Charles H. Bennett, David P. DiVincenzo, John A. Smolin. 1997. Research paper

    Exact erasure-channel capacities, distinguishing assistance models.

  44. From Classical to Quantum Shannon Theory

    Mark M. Wilde. 2011. Textbook manuscript

    Coding tasks, coherent information, regularization, and converse bounds.

  45. Quantum Communication With Zero-Capacity Channels

    Graeme Smith, Jon Yard. 2008. Research paper

    Superactivation: joint use of two zero-capacity channels can transmit quantum information.

  46. A Theory of Quantum Error-Correcting Codes

    Emanuel Knill, Raymond Laflamme. 1996. Research paper

    Necessary and sufficient conditions for exact correction on a code space.

  47. Quantum conditional mutual information and approximate Markov chains

    Omar Fawzi, Renato Renner. 2014. Research paper

    Conditional mutual information bounds the fidelity of an approximate recovery.

  48. Robust randomized benchmarking of quantum processes

    Easwar Magesan, J. M. Gambetta, Joseph Emerson. 2010. Research paper

    Develops a protocol for estimating average gate errors with stated assumptions about noise.

  49. Quantum Computing in the NISQ era and beyond

    John Preskill. 2018. Perspective

    Frames the relationship between noisy processors, useful computation, and fault tolerance.

  50. Quantum error correction below the surface code threshold

    Rajeev Acharya et al.. 2024. Experimental research

    Reports surface-code memories with improving logical performance as code distance increases in the tested regime.

  51. Fundamental Limits of Repeaterless Quantum Communications

    Stefano Pirandola, Riccardo Laurenza, Carlo Ottaviani, Leonardo Banchi. 2015. Research paper

    Two-way assisted capacity benchmarks for pure-loss and other channels; specify modes and assistance.

  52. Quantum repeaters: From quantum networks to the quantum internet

    Koji Azuma et al.. 2022. Review

    Connects repeater architectures with the challenge of distributing quantum information over long distances.

  53. Quantum sensing

    C. L. Degen, F. Reinhard, P. Cappellaro. 2016. Review

    Introduces quantum sensing methods and how physical signals become measurable quantum dynamics.

  54. Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum metrology using quantum error correction

    Sisi Zhou et al.. 2017. Research paper

    Derives conditions for error-corrected sensing under a specified noise and control model.
