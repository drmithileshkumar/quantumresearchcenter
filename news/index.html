  1. Research paper · Selected 29 October 2026

    Certification Time for Weak-Force-Mediated Entanglement between Levitated Particles

    Experiments with levitated nanoparticles aim to show that a weak force, and eventually gravity, entangles the motion of two particles. Producing the entangled state is only half the task: it has to be told apart from separable states using a finite number of noisy measurements, taken one run at a time. How many measurements, and how much laboratory time, does certification need, and which protocol reaches the weakest forces?

  2. Research paper · Selected 29 October 2026

    Qubit-environment entanglement in pure dephasing dynamics for mixed initial states

    When a qubit only dephases, the joint qubit–environment state for a pure initial qubit is separable exactly when the environment ends up in the same state whichever pointer state the qubit is in, \hat R_{00}(t)=\hat R_{11}(t). This test made it possible to tell decoherence by entanglement from decoherence by classical correlations, and even to detect the difference on the qubit alone. Real qubits are already partly mixed. Does the test survive, and does entanglement then evolve as it does for pure qubits?

  3. Research paper · Selected 29 October 2026

    Meet-in-the-Middle Compilation of the [CNOT] Class with Fibonacci Anyons

    Two qubits encoded in six Fibonacci anyons live in a five-dimensional fusion space, one dimension more than two qubits need. The only exchange that entangles the qubits, \sigma_3, is also the only one that couples them to the fifth, noncomputational state, so every entangling braid leaks. How small can the leakage of a braid equivalent to CNOT up to single-qubit gates be at a given length, and is exhaustive search better than random sampling?
